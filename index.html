Permits first

Indian travellers need an Inner Line Permit for Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Mizoram and Manipur. Sikkim's high lakes, Lakshadweep and some border areas of Ladakh need permits too. Foreign visitors need separate permits for several areas. Apply before you book transport.

Respect the height

Above roughly 3,000 m, gain height slowly. Headache, nausea or breathlessness at rest means stop climbing and go down. Chitkul, Spiti, Ladakh and Chopta all reward an extra rest day.

Book real operators

For rafting, paragliding, diving and caving, choose registered operators with trained guides, helmets and life jackets. Skip anyone who offers to run a rapid or cave after heavy rain.
